# Sources

Research was carried out on 24 October 2026. The research environment could run web searches but could not open most full-text pages or PDFs (outbound fetches to journal and organisation sites were blocked). Every source below was therefore verified at **abstract, official summary or listing level only** unless stated otherwise. Anything that depends on full-text detail is marked as needing confirmation, and nothing here should be read as a claim that the full paper was reviewed.

Evidence quality scale used throughout:

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Position stand, umbrella review, or systematic review / meta-analysis |
| **B** | Randomised or controlled trial |
| **C** | Observational, biomechanical or lab study |
| **D** | Recognised coaching convention or community resource (not research) |
| **E** | Product listing, review site, or vendor claim (unverified) |
